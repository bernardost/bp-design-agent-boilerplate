# The design-brief questions

`/brief` copies the sections that fit the project into `brain/project-brief.md` and answers
each question from the context it has. This file is the one list; the brief holds the answers.

Adapted from three published brief templates: Asana's design brief guide
(asana.com/resources/design-brief), JVDS's UI design brief template
(jvds.cn/en/share/ui-design/ui-design-brief-template) and the Black Country Chamber's six
brief questions (blackcountrychamber.co.uk). They cover the business problem, the audience,
the brand, the product's scope, the constraints and who decides, not only what it should
look like.

## How a question is written in the brief

```
- ★ **Why is this happening now, and what is not working today?** Signups fell 30% after the competitor launched. ^[Dana · kickoff · 2026-08-11 14:20](https://…)
- **What does success look like in 12 months?** Probably expansion into the US. ^[inferred]
- ★ **Who makes the final call?** ? **[Dana]** She mentioned a board, unclear who sits on it.
- **What content will exist at design time?** n/a — no product UI in this engagement.
```

| The answer | The page shows |
| --- | --- |
| text with a source tag | answered |
| text carrying `^[inferred]` | inferred: check it first |
| `?`, optionally an owner tag `**[who]**` and a note | missing |
| `n/a — why` | not applicable |

★ marks a key question: the work should not move far without its answer. Keep every ★
question in a section you keep. A whole section that does not apply stays as its heading
and one line, `n/a — why`, so the page shows it was considered.

## The opening question

Ask it first in any kickoff:

> Describe the experience you want a customer to have, from first seeing the brand to
> completing their most important task. What should they feel, understand and be able to do?

It makes stakeholders connect brand, product, audience and business goals before anyone
debates colours, logos or layout.

## Business and purpose

- ★ **Why is this project happening now, and what is not working today?**
- ★ **What business outcomes should the work drive — signups, retention, conversion, trust, pricing power, expansion?**
- ★ **What does success look like at launch, at 3 months and at 12 months?**
- **What is the company's mission, positioning and main point of difference?**
- **What are the biggest risks or misconceptions about the company or product?**
- ★ **What must not change — name, logo, core colours, product promise, legal claims, customer expectations?**

## Audience and context

- ★ **Who are the primary, secondary and anti-audiences?**
- ★ **What are users trying to get done, and what frustrates them today?**
- **Where do people first meet the brand — app store, social, referral, sales call, search, the website?**
- **What devices, environments, languages, accessibility needs and levels of digital confidence must the design serve?**
- **What should users think, feel and do after their first interaction?**
- **What evidence exists — analytics, support tickets, interviews, sales objections, churn reasons, competitor feedback?**

## Brand strategy

- ★ **Which 3–5 adjectives describe the brand today, and which 3–5 describe where it needs to go?**
- **If the brand were a person, how would it speak, behave and make decisions?**
- **What emotional response should it create — confidence, calm, delight, urgency, authority, play?**
- ★ **What is the one idea the audience should remember?**
- **Which brand attributes are non-negotiable, and which are open to reinvention?**
- ★ **How should the brand differ from direct and indirect competitors, in tone and behaviour as well as looks?**

## Visual language

- ★ **Are we evolving an existing identity or creating a new one?**
- ★ **Which existing assets can be reused — guidelines, logo files, fonts, imagery, illustration, photography, icons?**
- **Which visual references does the client admire, and why — colour, type, density, motion, photography, layout, personality?**
- **Which references should be avoided, and why?**
- **Should it feel premium, technical, human, bold, minimal, warm, playful, or something else?**
- **How far must the system stretch — marketing, product UI, sales decks, social, packaging, events, future sub-brands?**
- **Which assets are needed — logo suite, colour tokens, type scale, icon set, illustration style, photography direction, motion principles, guidelines?**

## Product and UI scope

- ★ **Which surfaces are in scope — marketing site, onboarding, dashboard, checkout, settings, emails, empty states, errors, notifications?**
- ★ **Which user flows matter for launch, and which can wait?**
- **Which screens, states and edge cases must be designed — loading, empty, error, permission, offline, success, accessibility modes?**
- ★ **Is this a redesign, a new product, or an extension of a design system?**
- **What content, copy, data and imagery will exist at design time?**
- **Are there localisation, right-to-left, dark mode, breakpoint or accessibility requirements?**
- **Which components already exist, and are we designing a reusable system or one-off screens?**

## UX and interaction

- ★ **What are the three tasks a user must complete with the least friction?**
- **Where do users drop off, get confused or need support today?**
- **What information hierarchy should guide each key screen?**
- **How should the interface balance simplicity with depth for power users?**
- **Which interaction patterns does the category expect, and where is it worth breaking convention?**
- **How should motion and feedback help people understand, rather than decorate?**

## Technical and operational constraints

- ★ **Which platforms, frameworks, CMS, design tools and component libraries are in use?**
- **Which browser, OS, device and performance constraints apply?**
- ★ **Who builds the UI, and how closely must the designs follow implementation constraints?**
- **Which legal, regulatory, privacy, security or industry requirements affect the design?**
- **How will content and translations be maintained after launch?**

## Scope, process and decisions

- ★ **What is the first deliverable, and the bar it must clear?**
- ★ **Which deliverables are expected, in what formats, and by when?**
- ★ **What is explicitly out of scope?**
- **What is the budget range, and what trade-offs does it imply?**
- **Which dates are fixed, and which milestones can move?**
- ★ **Who makes the final decision, who gives input, and who approves each phase?**
- **How many review rounds are included, and what feedback format works best?**
- **What would make the team call this project unsuccessful?**
