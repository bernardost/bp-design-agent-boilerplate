# Lens · house

*How anything generated for the owner to read should look: `feed.html`, `brief.html`, the
exploration spreads, the desk, a rendered page or document. `/reviewer house <scope>`, and the
standard a renderer is built to.*

**This governs artifacts made for the owner, and nothing else.** It is not the product's
aesthetic and is never inherited by the work. What a project should look like is a project
decision, explored with `/explore` and judged against `brain/lenses/craft.md`. Applying these
rules to the work by default would substitute the workspace's taste for the project's, which
is exactly the safe average good design has to beat.

The writing rules for these pages are not here. They live in `AGENTS.md`, because they also
govern every reply and everything that goes to a client.

## 1 · What to look at

The rendered page at the width it will be read. Open it; do not infer it from the source.

## 2 · What to refuse to look at

Nothing in particular — this lens is about the surface, and the surface is all it judges.

## 3 · What it is judged on

- **Inter** for titles and body, a real monospace for code. Comfortable sizes, generous
  leading.
- **White background, black text.** Commit to it: no `prefers-color-scheme` block that flips
  the page to dark at the whim of an OS setting.
- **Structure with white space and hairline rules**, never rounded cards with drop shadows.
  Confident typographic hierarchy, a comfortable measure, letterspaced uppercase for small
  metadata labels.
- **Colour carries intent only.** Committing is ink, discarding is the one red on the page,
  references are the one habitual blue.
- **Evidence is present and quiet.** Where a page states something from outside this repo, the
  source is one hover away and never in the sentence — a faint superscript numeral, the full
  attribution at the foot. A page that prints its provenance inline is one nobody finishes; a
  page with none is one nobody can check.

The standard to hold it to: **an architecture magazine, not a generic AI-generated dark-mode
dashboard.** It is an interface for consuming information with attention, and it should be
interesting to look at.

## 4 · What is explicitly not the standard here

The project's own work, a client deliverable, and anything the owner has art-directed. Ask
before departing from this lens; do not split the difference.
